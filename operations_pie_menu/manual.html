Operations Pie Menu

Blender-style 8-sector Radial Pie Menu for Krita canvas and layer workflow automation.

Sectors

  • North (N) - Refine Sketch: Applies random HSL coloration to sketch lines, merges via luminosity, creates incremented layer +1, and activates '0 STD DRW' brush.
  • North East (NE) - Sanitize Group: Purges empty non-protected layers, adds fresh top drawing layer, keeps B&W layer at absolute top, and renumbers layers 1..N.
  • East (E) - Stub East: Reserved placeholder slot.
  • South East (SE) - B&W Preview: Toggles or creates top-level value checking layer.
  • South (S) - Init Canvas: Resets canvas to WHITE base layer (75% opacity) and LINES group with layer 1, activating '0 STD DRW' brush.
  • South West (SW) - Merge to Black: Merges non-protected paint layers inside target group into a solid black silhouette layer, strictly preserving WHITE at bottom [0], silhouette layer in middle, and B&W at top [-1]. Activates '0 STD DRW' brush.
  • West (W) - Fit Layer to Canvas: Scales active layer to canvas dimensions (respecting Keep Aspect Ratio condition).
  • North West (NW) - Stub North West: Reserved placeholder slot.
