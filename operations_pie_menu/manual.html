Operations Pie Menu

Blender-style 4-sector Radial Pie Menu for Krita canvas workflow.

Features

  • North (N): Stub North
  • East (E): Stub East
  • West (W): Stub West
  • South (S): Init Canvas - Checks layers, prompts "Nuke Document?" if >1 layer, fills base with solid white, renames to "WHITE" (75% opacity), creates group "LINES" with paint layer "1", and activates the Freehand Brush tool.
