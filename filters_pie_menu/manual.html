Filters Pie Menu

Blender-style 8-sector radial Pie Menu mapped to the Space key for instant access to Krita adjustment filters.

Controls & Interaction

  • Hold & Release: Hold Space, move mouse in a sector direction (blue highlight), and release Space to trigger the filter.
  • Neutral Deadzone: Releasing Space inside the center circle closes the menu without executing any filter.
  • Interrupt / Cancel: Press F11, Esc, or Right-Click to cancel the pie menu.

Filter Sector Layout

  • North (Up): HSV Adjustment...
  • North-East: Color Curves...
  • East (Right): Color Balance...
  • South-East: Slope, Offset, Power...
  • South (Down): Desaturate...
  • South-West: Auto Contrast
  • West (Left): Levels...
  • North-West: Invert

Customizing Sector Layouts

To move filters around or reassign sectors, open Tools > Scripts > Configure Filters Pie Menu inside Krita to launch the interactive Sector Layout Configurator.
