Conditions Pie Menu

Blender-style 8-sector radial Pie Menu mapped to Ctrl+Tab for managing workflow condition toggles and state flags.

Controls & Interaction

  • Hold & Release: Hold Ctrl+Tab, move cursor in a sector direction, and release to toggle or trigger a condition.
  • Neutral Deadzone: Releasing inside the center circle closes the menu without modifying state.
  • Interrupt / Cancel: Press F11, Esc, or Right-Click to cancel.

Conditions & Toggles

  • North-East (NE): Duplicate RefLay toggle (Controls automatic duplication of reference layers during sketch refinement).
  • Other Sectors: Configurable condition stubs.
